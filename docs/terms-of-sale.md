## Prices

All prices are in US dollars. Prices can change without notice, but the price you see when you place your order is the price you pay. Sale prices apply while stock lasts and until the sale ends.

We make every effort to display prices correctly. If we find a pricing mistake after you order, we will contact you before shipping and offer you the choice of confirming the order at the correct price or cancelling it for a full refund.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout from the delivery address, using the rate for that state and local area. Oregon has no state sales tax, so orders delivered to Oregon addresses are not charged any. For other states the tax shown at checkout is the amount charged. Tax is shown as a separate line, and is not part of the item price.

Orders delivered outside the US are not charged US sales tax. The buyer may owe import taxes, as explained on the international shipping page.

When you place an order, you receive an email confirming that we have received it. This is not a promise that we will supply the goods. A contract is formed when we ship your order and send the shipping email. We can decline or cancel an order for reasons such as a payment problem, suspected fraud, or a suspected pricing error.

## Stock and availability

Stock levels change quickly. We try to show accurate availability, but items can sell out while they are in someone's bag.

### Out of stock after payment

Sometimes an item turns out to be out of stock after you have paid, for example if two people buy the last one at the same moment, or if a stock count was wrong. If this happens, we email you within 2 business days. You can choose to:

1. wait for the item to be restocked, if we expect a restock date
2. swap to another item or size of similar value
3. cancel that item and receive a full refund to your original payment method

If you do not reply within 5 business days, we cancel the unavailable item and refund it. The rest of your order ships as normal, unless you ask us to hold it.
