# Refunds

This page explains how refunds are paid, how long they take and what is and is not refunded. Whether an item can be returned in the first place is covered on the returns and exchanges page.

## When a refund is issued

We issue a refund after we have received your returned item and checked it. We check parcels within 2 business days of arrival. If the item meets our conditions, we start the refund the same day. If it does not, we contact you before doing anything.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the payment method you used for the order. We cannot refund to a different card, account or wallet.

| Original payment      | Refund goes to                                                | Typical timing        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Credit or debit card  | The same card                                                 | 5–10 business days    |
| Digital wallet        | The same wallet                                               | 5–10 business days    |
| Pay-in-4 installments | The installment provider, who adjusts your remaining payments | 5–10 business days    |
| Gift card             | Store credit only (see below)                                 | Within 1 business day |

The 5 to 10 business days start when we process the refund. After that your bank or provider controls the timing, and some banks take a little longer to show it. If you have not seen the money after 10 business days, check with your bank, then contact us.

## Orders paid with a gift card

If your order was paid for with a gift card, in whole or in part, we cannot refund that portion to a card or in cash. It is returned as store credit instead. Store credit is added to your account and can be used on any future order. It does not expire.

If you paid partly by card and partly with a gift card, the card portion goes back to the card, and the gift card portion becomes store credit.

## What is refunded

For a normal return, we refund the price of the item. We also refund sales tax charged on that item.

- The original shipping charge is not refunded when you return an item because you changed your mind.
- The return label cost is deducted, as explained on the returns page.
- International customs charges are not refunded by us.

If your item arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, the position is different: we refund the full price, sales tax and the original shipping charge, and you do not pay for the return. See the damaged or wrong item page.

## Partial returns

If you return only some of the items in an order, we refund those items only. If the remaining items no longer qualify for free shipping, we do not charge you extra for that.

## Discount codes and loyalty points

When you return an item bought with a discount code, we refund the amount you actually paid, not the full price. Loyalty points earned on a returned item are removed from your account. If this leaves your balance below zero, the next points you earn will cover it.

## Store credit

Store credit is issued when:

- an exchange cannot be completed because the item is out of stock
- an order was paid with a gift card

You will receive an email confirming the amount. You can see your balance at checkout.

## Refund problems

If your refund is late or the amount looks wrong, text or email customer service with your order number. We can see when the refund was sent and which method was used. Our contact details are on the about us page.
