# About Tallow & Pine

Tallow & Pine is an online women's clothing store based in Portland, Oregon. We started in 2019 in a small studio with a handful of dresses and a simple idea: well-made everyday clothes, clear prices, and honest answers when something goes wrong. This page explains who we are and how to reach us.

## Online only

We sell only through our website. We do not have retail stores, pop-up shops or a showroom, so there is nowhere to try clothes on or pick up an order. Everything ships from our Portland studio, and every policy on this site is written with online shoppers in mind. If you are unsure about fit, the size guide and the fabric and care page answer most questions before you order.

## Opening hours

Customer service is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm Pacific time. We are closed on weekends and on US public holidays. Messages that arrive outside these hours are answered on the next business day, in the order we received them.

| Channel      | Hours                    | Typical reply time                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Text or call | Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm Pacific | Within 2 hours during opening hours            |
| Email        | Always open for sending  | Within 1 business day                          |
| Instagram    | Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm Pacific | Same business day, then moved to text or email |

Texting is the fastest way to get an answer. Email is better when you need to send photos, for example to report a problem with an item.

## What we sell

Tallow & Pine designs and sells women's clothing in sizes XS to XL, in both regular and petite fits. Our range includes dresses, tops, knitwear, jackets, pants and skirts, in fabrics such as cotton, linen, wool blends and denim. New designs arrive every season, and popular items can sell out. When a size or color is gone, it may return in a later batch, but we cannot promise dates.
