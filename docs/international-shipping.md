# International shipping

Tallow & Pine ships outside the United States to a limited list of countries. This page explains where we deliver, how long it takes, what it costs and who pays customs charges.

## Countries we ship to

We currently deliver to:

- Canada
- The United Kingdom
- Member countries of the European Union
- Australia

We do not ship to any other country at the moment, and we cannot arrange special deliveries or use freight forwarders. If your country is not listed, it will not be available at checkout. The list may grow in future, but we do not publish plans.

## Delivery times

International orders take 7 to 14 business days from the day they ship. Orders usually leave our Portland studio within 1 business day, as with US orders. The wide range exists because customs clearance varies by country and by time of year, and parcels can be held at the border for inspection. Delivery times are estimates, not promises.

## Shipping cost

International shipping is a flat $29 per order, no matter how many items you buy. The free shipping offer that applies to US orders does not apply to international orders. The $29 is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Customs, duties and taxes

Customs duties, import taxes and any handling fees charged by the destination country are paid by the buyer. They are not included in our prices or in the $29 shipping cost, and we cannot estimate them in advance because each country sets its own rules and thresholds.

Depending on the country, you may be asked to pay in one of two ways:

- **At delivery:** the carrier or customs office contacts you and asks for payment before releasing the parcel.
- **Afterwards:** a bill is sent to you by the carrier.

If a parcel is refused or left unpaid and is sent back to us, we cannot refund the shipping cost, and the cost of the return trip may be deducted from any refund.

## Customs forms

We fill in all customs paperwork accurately. We cannot lower the declared value or change the description of the contents, because that is not permitted. Please do not ask us to.

## Returns from outside the US

Returns are possible for international orders, and the usual returns policy applies. You pay for return postage from your country. Duties and import taxes you paid on delivery are not refunded by us, but you may be able to reclaim them from your local customs office. The returns and refunds pages explain the conditions.

## Tracking

We email and text a tracking link when your order ships. Once the parcel reaches your country, tracking may pause for a few days while it clears customs. This is normal. The order tracking page explains what to do if it does not move.

## Delivery address and contact details

Please give a full address in the format used in your country, and a phone number that the carrier can use, in case customs needs to reach you. A missing postcode or phone number is a common reason for international delays. We cannot change the address once an order has shipped.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether we deliver to you, or what a charge on delivery means, contact customer service. Our details are on the about us page.
