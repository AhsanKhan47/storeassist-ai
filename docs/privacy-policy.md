# Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information Tallow & Pine collects when you visit our site or place an order, why we collect it, who sees it and what choices you have.

## What we collect

When you buy from us or create an account, we collect:

- your name, email address and phone number
- delivery and billing addresses
- your order history and any messages you send to customer service
- loyalty points and store credit balances

When you visit the site, we also collect basic technical information, such as your device type, browser and the pages you view. We use cookies for this and to keep items in your bag.

We do not collect more than we need to process orders and answer your questions.

## Payment details

Card details are entered on our payment provider's secure page. We never see or store your full card number. We only keep the last four digits and the card type, so we can show you which card was used.

## How we use your information

We use your information to:

- process and ship your orders
- send order confirmations, shipping updates and tracking links by email and text
- answer your questions and handle returns, refunds and claims
- run our loyalty points scheme
- improve the site and our products
- prevent fraud

We send marketing emails only to people who have agreed to receive them. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not affect order emails.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with companies that help us run the store:

- shipping carriers, who need your name, address and phone number
- our payment provider and pay-in-4 provider
- email and text delivery services
- website hosting and analytics providers

These companies may use your information only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade mailing lists.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for 7 years, because tax rules require it. Account details are kept until you ask us to delete the account. Customer service messages are kept for 3 years. After that, we delete the information or remove anything that identifies you.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the information we hold about you
- correct information that is wrong
- delete your account and personal information, apart from records we must keep by law
- stop sending you marketing emails

Residents of California, and of the European Union and the United Kingdom, have specific legal rights over their data, and we will honor these. To make a request, email support@tallowandpine.example. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we aim to reply within 30 days.

## Children

Our site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Security

We use encryption on our site and limit who can see customer information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection, but we act quickly if there is a problem.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will update this page and email account holders. The date of the latest change is shown at the top of the page.

## Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@tallowandpine.example.
