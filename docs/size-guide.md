# Size guide

Tallow & Pine clothing is sold in sizes XS to XL, in two fits: regular and petite. This page explains how to measure yourself, how to read our size charts and which fit to choose.

## How to measure

Use a soft tape measure and wear light clothing. Keep the tape snug but not tight.

- **Bust:** around the fullest part of your chest.
- **Waist:** around the narrowest part of your waist, usually just above the belly button.
- **Hips:** around the widest part of your hips and seat.
- **Inseam:** from the top of your inner thigh to your ankle.

If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed fit, or the smaller for a closer fit. Each product page notes whether the item is cut close to the body or relaxed.

All measurements are in inches and describe your body, not the garment.

## Regular or petite

Petite sizes are designed for people who are 5 feet 4 inches (162 cm) or shorter. The width measurements in the chart above are the same for petite. What changes is the length:

- sleeves are about 1 inch shorter
- hems on dresses and skirts are about 2 inches shorter
- pant inseams are about 2 inches shorter

If you are taller than 5 feet 4 inches, choose regular. If you are shorter and would like a longer, more relaxed look, regular may suit you better. Petite is available in a smaller selection of items, and the product page shows which fits are offered.

## Garment measurements

Every product page lists the finished measurements of that item in inches, such as length, sleeve length and inseam. Compare these with a garment you already own and love. This is the most reliable way to check fit, particularly for jackets, knitwear and anything with a looser cut.

## Stretch and fabric

Fabric affects how a size feels:

- **Cotton and linen:** little stretch. Linen softens and relaxes over time.
- **Knitwear:** stretches with wear, so a close fit is expected at first.
- **Denim:** the rigid kind has no stretch and eases a little after a few wears. Stretch denim is noted on the product page.

## If your size does not fit

You can exchange an item for another size within 14 days, as long as it is unworn and the tags are on. The returns and exchanges page has the full conditions. If you would like the length changed instead, our alterations service can hem pants, skirts and dresses.

## Need help choosing

Text or email customer service with your measurements and the item you are interested in, and we will suggest a size. Our contact details are on the about us page.
