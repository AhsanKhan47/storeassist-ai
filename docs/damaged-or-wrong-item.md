# Damaged or wrong item

Sometimes an order arrives with a problem. This page explains what counts as damaged or wrong, how soon you must tell us, what to send us and what we will do.

## The 48-hour rule

You must report a damaged, faulty or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery. The 48 hours start from the delivery time shown in your tracking. We ask for a quick report because it is much easier to confirm what happened and to put it right while the parcel is fresh.

Reports made after 48 hours may not be eligible for a free replacement or refund under this page. If you are late, still contact us, and we will look at what we can do. Do not wait to see if the problem goes away.

## What counts as a problem

We treat these as problems:

- the item arrived torn, stained, broken or with a manufacturing fault
- the item arrived with a missing button, zipper or other part
- you received a different item, size or color from the one you ordered
- an item from your order is missing from the parcel
- the parcel was crushed or opened in transit and the contents are damaged

These are not treated as problems under this page: an item that does not fit, a color that looks different on your screen, or damage that happened after delivery, such as a snagged sweater or a stain from wear. For fit, see the returns and exchanges page.

## What to send us

Text or email customer service within the 48 hours, and include:

1. your order number
2. a short description of what is wrong
3. clear photos of the problem, showing the whole item and a close-up of the fault
4. a photo of the shipping label and the outside of the parcel, if the parcel itself was damaged

Photos are required. We cannot approve a claim without them. Email is the best way to send them. Please keep the item until we reply, and do not wash or wear it.

## What we will do

Once we have confirmed the problem, you can choose one of two outcomes:

- **Replacement:** we send you the same item in the same size and color, or a close alternative if it is out of stock, at no cost to you.
- **Refund:** we refund the full price of the item, the sales tax and the original shipping charge, to your original payment method.

We usually reply within 1 business day. In most cases you do not have to send the damaged item back, but we may ask to see it if the fault is unusual. If we ask for a return, we provide a prepaid label, and you do not pay for it.

## Wrong item

If you received the wrong item, we send the correct one as soon as it is available, and we email a prepaid label so you can send the wrong one back. If the correct item is out of stock, we refund your order in full.

## Missing items

Check the packing slip against what is in the parcel. Items sometimes ship separately, and we say so in the shipping email. If something is missing and there is no separate shipping notice, contact us within 48 hours.

## Parcels damaged by the carrier

If the parcel is visibly damaged on arrival, take photos of the outside before opening it. If the carrier is still there, you may refuse the parcel. We handle carrier claims on our side and you do not need to contact the carrier yourself.

## Contact

Contact details and hours are on the about us page. Texting is quickest for reporting a problem, then send photos by email.
