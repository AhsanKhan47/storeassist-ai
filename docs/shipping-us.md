# Shipping in the US

This page covers delivery within the United States: how long it takes, what it costs, and where we can and cannot deliver. For orders outside the US, see the international shipping page.

## How long delivery takes

Most orders leave our Portland studio within 1 business day of being placed. Delivery times below count business days from the day your order ships, not the day you order. Weekends and public holidays do not count.

| Delivery region                             | Estimated delivery time |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| West Coast (California, Oregon, Washington) | 2–3 business days       |
| Rest of the contiguous US                   | 3–6 business days       |
| Alaska, Hawaii and PO boxes                 | 7–10 business days      |

These are estimates provided by our shipping carriers, not guarantees. Severe weather and carrier delays can add time, and we cannot speed up a parcel once it is with the carrier.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is a flat $6.95 per order, however many items you buy. Orders of $75 or more ship free. The $75 is calculated on your item subtotal after discounts have been applied and before sales tax. If a discount code takes your order below $75, the $6.95 charge applies.

Shipping is charged once per order. If your order has to be sent in more than one parcel, you are not charged again.

## Where we deliver

We deliver to all 50 states, to PO boxes, and to APO and FPO military addresses. Parcels to Alaska, Hawaii and PO boxes travel part of the way with a different service, which is why they take longer.

We cannot change the delivery address once an order has shipped. If you notice a mistake before your order leaves the studio, contact customer service straight away and we will try to correct it.

## Signatures and missed deliveries

Orders do not require a signature. Our carriers leave parcels at your door or with a neighbor or building front desk where that is normal for your area. If a carrier cannot deliver, they usually leave a notice with instructions for collection or redelivery. Parcels that are not collected are returned to us, and we will contact you to arrange a reshipment or a refund.

If your tracking shows a parcel as delivered but you cannot find it, check around your property and with neighbors, then contact the carrier and us within 2 business days.

## One order or two

Each order ships on its own. We cannot combine two separate orders into one parcel after they have been placed, and a second order does not change the shipping charge on the first. If you want to buy several items, adding them to a single order is the cheapest way to reach the free shipping amount.

## Getting your tracking details

Once your order ships, we email and text you a tracking link. Tracking and delay information is explained on the order tracking page.

## Questions about delivery

If your order has not arrived within the estimate above, check the tracking link first. If the tracking has not moved, contact customer service with your order number. Our contact details and opening hours are on the about us page.
