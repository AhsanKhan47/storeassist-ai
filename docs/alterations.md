# Alterations

Tallow & Pine offers a hemming service for pants, skirts and dresses, so a garment can arrive at the length you want. This page explains what is offered, what it costs, how long it takes and how to order it.

## What we offer

We offer hemming only. We shorten the bottom edge of pants, skirts and dresses to a length you choose, and we finish the hem in a way that matches the original as closely as possible. We do not offer other changes such as taking in a waist, adjusting sleeves, adding lining or changing the shape of a garment.

Hemming is not available on every item. The product page shows a hemming option where it can be done. Garments with detailed hems, such as pleats, lace edges or printed borders, are usually not eligible.

## Cost

Hemming costs $25 per item. If you order hemming on three pairs of pants, the cost is $75. The fee is added to your order total and paid at checkout, along with the price of the garments.

The alteration fee does not count towards the free shipping amount. Free shipping is calculated on the item price only, as explained on the shipping page.

## Time

Hemming adds 7 to 10 business days before your order ships. The delivery times on the shipping page begin after this, once the order leaves the studio. Because of this, an order with a hemmed item arrives later than an order without one, and the tracking link arrives later too.

If an order contains both hemmed and unaltered items, the whole order ships together once the hemming is finished. We cannot send the unaltered items ahead.

## Not available on sale items

Hemming cannot be added to items bought at a reduced sale price. The option does not appear on sale product pages. If you would like a sale item shortened, you will need to have it altered elsewhere after it arrives.

## How to order a hem

1. Choose the item and your size, as usual.
2. Select the hemming option on the product page.
3. Enter the length you want in inches. For pants, give the inseam. For skirts and dresses, give the length from the waist or shoulder to the hem.
4. Check the length, add the item to your bag and complete the order.

## Measuring for a hem

The most dependable method is to measure a garment you already own that fits well, laid flat. Measure the same part on both items and use that number. Stand up straight and have someone else measure if you can, because bending changes the result.

The shortest length we can hem to is shown on each product page. We cannot add length to a garment. If you are unsure, ask customer service for help before you order, using the contact details on the about us page.

## Changing or cancelling a hem

Once the studio starts work on a hem, it cannot be changed or cancelled. Contact customer service as soon as possible if you notice a mistake in the length you entered. We start work on hemming the business day after your order is placed.

## Fit problems after a hem

We make each hem to the length you enter, so please double-check your numbers before checkout. If the hem was made incorrectly, meaning it does not match the length you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and a measurement.
