# Order tracking

This page explains how to follow your order, what the tracking statuses mean, and when to expect delays.

## Getting your tracking link

Within 24 hours of your order shipping, we email and text you a tracking link. The email goes to the address you used at checkout, and the text goes to the phone number you gave us. If you cannot find either, check your spam folder, then contact customer service with your order number.

Your order confirmation email, sent when you place the order, is not the same as the shipping email. It has no tracking link, because the parcel has not left the studio yet.

## What the statuses mean

| Status             | What it means                                                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Label created      | The carrier has our shipping label, but has not yet collected the parcel |
| In transit         | The parcel is moving between carrier facilities                          |
| Out for delivery   | The parcel is on the vehicle that will deliver it today                  |
| Delivered          | The carrier has left the parcel at your address                          |
| Delivery exception | Something has gone wrong, such as a bad address or weather delay         |

It is normal for a parcel to stay on "label created" for up to a day. Tracking can also go quiet for a day or two while a parcel travels between regions.

## Holiday peak delays

From November 20 to December 24, our carriers handle far more parcels than usual. During this period, add 3 to 5 business days to any delivery estimate you see on this site or at checkout. This applies to both US and international orders. Orders placed during this period still ship from our studio within 1 business day, but they move more slowly once they are with the carrier.

If you need an order to arrive by a certain date, please order well before November 20. We cannot guarantee arrival dates at any time of year.

## If your tracking has not moved

Wait until at least 5 business days have passed with no update, then contact customer service. During the holiday peak, please wait 7 business days. Have your order number and tracking number ready. We will contact the carrier on your behalf and let you know what we find.

## If tracking says delivered, but nothing arrived

First, check around your property, with neighbors and with anyone else in your household who may have taken it in. Parcels are sometimes marked delivered a few hours before they arrive. If you still cannot find it after one business day, contact the carrier and then us, within 2 business days of the delivery date.

## Delivery exceptions

If a carrier reports an exception, they will often try again the next day. If they cannot deliver, the parcel may be held at a depot or returned to us. We will contact you to arrange a reshipment or a refund. We cannot change the delivery address once a parcel has shipped.

## Order status before shipping

Until your order ships, we cannot give a tracking number. Orders ship within 1 business day on weekdays. Orders placed over the weekend ship on the next business day. Where an alteration was requested, the order ships after the alteration is finished.

## Contact

Our contact details and hours are on the about us page. Texting is the quickest way to ask about an order.
