# Discounts and loyalty

This page explains how discount codes work, where they can and cannot be used, and how our loyalty points scheme works.

## Discount codes

Discount codes are entered in the box at checkout. Each code has its own conditions, shown when it is issued, such as a minimum spend or an expiry date. A code that has expired or whose conditions are not met will be rejected at checkout.

### Where codes come from

We publish codes only through our email newsletter and our official social media accounts. Customer service cannot create custom codes, extend expired ones or give out codes on request, and codes found on third-party coupon sites may not be valid.

### One code per order

You can use one discount code per order. Codes cannot be stacked, and they cannot be combined with each other, even if both are valid. If you have two codes, choose the one that gives you the better saving. An order cannot be split in order to use two codes, because a code applies to the whole of the order it is entered on.

### Codes do not apply to sale items

Discount codes cannot be used on items that are already at a reduced sale price. If your bag contains both sale and full-price items, the code applies only to the full-price items. Items at a sale price are excluded from any minimum spend that a code requires.

### Adding a code after you order

We cannot add a discount code to an order once it has been placed. If you forget to enter a code, you will need to contact customer service, and we cannot promise a different outcome.

## Loyalty points

Everyone who creates an account earns loyalty points on their orders automatically.

- You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend.
- Points are calculated on the amount you pay after discounts, and before shipping and sales tax.
- Items bought at a sale price earn points like any other item.
- 100 points are worth $5 off a future order.

### Using your points

You can redeem points at checkout in blocks of 100. Points are not a discount code, so you can redeem them on the same order as a code. Points can be used on full-price and sale items. You see your balance in your account and at checkout.

| Points | Value   |
| ------ | ------- |
| 100    | $5 off  |
| 200    | $10 off |
| 500    | $25 off |

### Expiry

Points expire 12 months after the day you earned them. The oldest points are used first. We send a reminder email about 30 days before points expire, but it is your responsibility to check your balance.

### Returns and points

If you return an item, the points you earned on it are removed. If you have already spent those points, your balance may go below zero, and your next points cover the difference. Points used on a returned order are not restored.

## Gift cards are not discounts

A gift card is a form of payment, not a discount. You can use a gift card on the same order as a code and loyalty points. Gift cards do not earn points when they are bought, but orders paid for with them do earn points on the amount paid.

## Mistakes and abuse

We may cancel points or codes that were issued by mistake, or obtained by creating several accounts. We will tell you if we do this.

## Questions

For help with a code that will not work, text or email customer service. Our details are on the about us page.
