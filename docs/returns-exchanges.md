# Returns and exchanges

We want you to be happy with your order. If something is not right, this page explains how returns and exchanges work, which items qualify and what condition they must be in.

## The 14-day return window

You can return or exchange an item within 14 days of the day it was delivered. The 14 days are calendar days, counted from the delivery date shown in your tracking. We must receive your request, not the parcel itself, within this window. Once approved, you then have a few days to post the item back.

## Condition of returned items

To qualify, an item must be:

- unworn and unwashed
- free of perfume, smoke, pet hair and marks
- in its original condition with all tags still attached

Items that show signs of wear, washing or damage caused after delivery are refused. If we refuse an item, we send it back to you at your cost, or hold it for 30 days if you ask us to.

## Sale items are exchange only

Items bought at a reduced sale price can be exchanged but not returned for a refund. An exchange means swapping for the same item in a different size or color, subject to stock. If your size or color is not available, we issue store credit for the price you paid, instead of a refund to your card.

## Altered items cannot be returned

Items that have been altered by us cannot be returned or exchanged, because the change cannot be reversed. Please check your measurements before ordering an alteration.

## How to start a return or exchange

1. Text or email customer service with your order number and the item you want to return.
2. Tell us whether you want a refund (where allowed), an exchange, or store credit.
3. We approve the request and email you a prepaid return label.
4. Pack the item securely, attach the label and drop it with the carrier.

Do not post an item back before we have approved it, as we may not be able to match it to your order.

## Who pays for return shipping

For returns, the cost of the prepaid label is $6.95, and we deduct it from your refund. Exchanges are different: we pay for the label, and we send the replacement to you at no charge. If an item is damaged or the wrong item, you do not pay for return shipping. The damaged or wrong item page explains what to do in that case.

## What happens next

When your parcel arrives, we check the item against the conditions above. We usually do this within 2 business days. If the item passes, we process your refund, exchange or store credit. The refunds page explains how refunds are paid and how long they take.

## Items we cannot take back

Besides worn items and altered items, we cannot accept returns of:

- items returned after the 14-day window
- items with the tags removed
- gift cards

If you are unsure whether an item qualifies, contact customer service before you send it. We would rather tell you now than after you have paid to post it.
