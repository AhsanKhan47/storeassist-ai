# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions customers ask most. Each answer links to a fuller page where there is more detail, and the full page is always the one to rely on.

## Orders and delivery

### How much does shipping cost?

Standard US shipping is $6.95 per order. Orders of $75 or more ship free. The US shipping page has the details.

### How long will my order take?

Most US orders arrive within 2 to 6 business days of shipping. Alaska, Hawaii and PO boxes take 7 to 10 business days. Orders usually leave our studio within 1 business day.

### How do I track my order?

We email and text you a tracking link within 24 hours of your order shipping. If you cannot find it, check your spam folder or contact customer service.

### Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. International shipping is $29 per order and takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the buyer.

### Can I change my delivery address?

Only before the order ships. Contact customer service as soon as you notice a mistake. After shipping, we cannot change the address.

## Returns and refunds

### How long do I have to return something?

You have 14 days from delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and have the tags attached. The returns and exchanges page lists the full conditions.

### How long does a refund take?

Once we process it, refunds to cards and wallets take 5 to 10 business days to reach you.

### What if my item arrives damaged?

Text or email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will replace the item or refund you. The damaged or wrong item page has the steps.

## Sizes and care

### How do I choose a size?

Compare your measurements with the size chart, and check the finished measurements on the product page. Petite fits are for people 5 feet 4 inches or shorter. If you are between sizes, text us your measurements and we will suggest one.

### Can I hem pants or a dress?

Yes. Hemming costs $25 per item and is offered on pants, skirts and dresses where the product page shows the option. See the alterations page for the details.

### Will my new jeans color run?

Dark denim can release dye on its first wash. Wash it alone in cold water. The fabric and care page explains this and other care advice.

## Payments and discounts

### Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, digital wallets, gift cards and pay-in-4 installments. All prices are in US dollars.

### Can I pay in installments?

Yes, pay-in-4 is available on orders up to $500. The first payment is taken at checkout.

### How do discount codes work?

You can use one discount code per order. Codes cannot be combined. We publish them through our newsletter and official social media accounts.

### How do loyalty points work?

You earn 1 point for every $1 you spend, and 100 points are worth $5 off. Points expire after 12 months.

We are an online store based in Portland, Oregon. We do not have retail locations.
