# Payment methods

This page explains how you can pay for an order at Tallow & Pine, when you are charged, and how pay-in-4 installments work.

## What we accept

We accept the following at checkout:

- major credit and debit cards
- digital wallets
- Tallow & Pine gift cards
- pay-in-4 installments

We do not accept checks, money orders, cash, bank transfers or cryptocurrency. All prices and charges are in US dollars. If your card is issued outside the US, your bank may add a foreign transaction fee, which we do not control.

## When you are charged

Your payment is taken when you place the order. If the payment is declined, the order is not placed, and you can try again with another method. If an item turns out to be out of stock after you have paid, the terms of sale page explains what happens.

Your statement will show the charge as Tallow & Pine. If you do not recognize a charge, please contact us before disputing it with your bank, as we can usually explain it quickly.

## Pay-in-4 installments

Pay-in-4 lets you split an order into four equal payments. The first is taken at checkout, and the other three follow every two weeks.

- It is available on orders of up to $500. Larger orders must be paid in full with another method.
- There is no extra charge from us. The installment provider carries out its own approval check, and late payments may carry fees under their terms.
- If you return part of an order, the refund reduces the remaining payments first. The refunds page has more detail.

Approval is decided by the provider, not by Tallow & Pine, and we cannot influence the result. If you are declined, you can choose another way to pay.

## Gift cards

You can pay for all or part of an order with a gift card by entering its code at checkout. If the gift card does not cover the whole order, you pay the balance with another method. Unused balance stays on the card.

Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash. If a gift card is used to pay for an order that is later refunded, the money goes back as store credit, as explained on the refunds page.

## Using more than one method

You can combine a gift card with one other payment method. You cannot split an order across two cards, and you cannot combine pay-in-4 with a gift card on the same order.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is added at checkout based on the delivery address. The terms of sale page explains how it works.

## Security

Card details are entered on our payment provider's secure page, not on our own pages. We never see or store your full card number. If you spot a charge you did not make, contact your bank first, then let us know.

## Problems with a payment

Common reasons for a declined payment are a mistyped card number, an address that does not match the one your bank has, or a limit on your card. Try again, or use another method. If the problem persists, contact customer service with your order number. Our details are on the about us page.
