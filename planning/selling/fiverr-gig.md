## Description

**Your customers ask the same questions every day. Let an AI assistant answer them, correctly.**

I'll build a support chatbot that answers questions about returns, shipping, sizing, payments and more,
**using only your own policy documents**. Every answer shows its source, and when something isn't in
your policies it says so and gives your contact details instead of making things up.

**See it working before you order:** https://storeassist-ai.pages.dev
(a demo for a fictional clothing store: try "Can I return a sale item?" or "Do you sell shoes?")

**What you get**

- An AI assistant trained on your FAQ, returns, shipping and other policy pages
- A source link under every answer, so customers can check it
- No made-up answers: off-topic or unknown questions get your contact details
- Protection against people trying to trick it into fake discounts or ignoring its rules
- A floating chat button for your website, added with **one line of code** (Shopify, WordPress, Wix,
  Webflow or a custom site), in your brand color
- A test report: real customer questions checked against the bot before handover
- Hosting on free plans in your own accounts: no monthly fees for normal small-store traffic

**How it works**

1. You send your policy pages (website links, PDFs or Word files)
2. I prepare the documents, build and test the assistant
3. You get a test link, ask it anything, and request changes
4. I add it to your website and hand over everything

**Built with:** Next.js, Cloudflare Workers, Postgres with vector search, Groq and Google Gemini
models. Full source code included.

Message me before ordering if you're not sure which package fits. I'm happy to look at your site first.

## FAQ

**Will it make up answers?**
No. It only answers from your documents. If the best match isn't close enough, it doesn't call the AI
model at all and gives your contact details instead. Every answer shows the source it used.

**Do I need to pay for hosting or AI every month?**
It runs on free plans (Cloudflare, Neon, Upstash, Groq, Google Gemini) in your own accounts. Free plans
have limits; for a busy store a paid tier may be needed (Cloudflare Workers starts at $5/month). I'll
explain the limits before you order.

**Does it work with Shopify / WordPress / Wix?**
Yes. The chat button is one script tag, which all of these allow. You can try it on the demo store page:
https://storeassist-ai.pages.dev/demo/

**Can it look up orders or process refunds?**
Not in these packages: it answers policy questions. Live order lookup can be added as a custom order;
message me with your platform.

**What if my policies change?**
You edit the document and re-run one command, or order the monthly update extra and I do it.

**Can I see the code?**
Yes, the demo's full code is public on GitHub, and you get the full source for your build.
