## FAQ

**Will it make up answers?**
No. It only answers from your documents. If nothing in them is a close enough match, it skips the AI
entirely and gives your contact details. Every answer shows the source it used.

**Do I need to pay for hosting or AI every month?**
It runs on free plans (Cloudflare, Neon, Upstash, Groq, Google Gemini) in your own accounts. Free plans
have limits, so a very busy store may need a paid tier; Cloudflare's starts at $5/month. I'll explain
the limits before you order.

**Does it work with Shopify, WordPress or Wix?**
Yes. The chat button is a single script tag, which all of these platforms allow. I can add it to your
site for you, or send you the line to paste yourself.

**Can it look up orders or process refunds?**
Not in these packages: it answers policy and FAQ questions. Live order lookup can be added as a custom
order. Message me with your store platform and I'll quote it.

**What if my policies change?**
You edit the document and run one update command, and the assistant starts using the new version. I'll
show you how at handover, or I can make updates for you. You also get the full source code for your
build.
